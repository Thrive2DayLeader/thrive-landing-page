Elevate Delaware Funding

How Elevate Delaware funding actually works — step by step.

Elevate Delaware funding is run by the Delaware Department of Labor, not by us — but you don't have to figure out the paperwork alone. Here's exactly what the state asks for, in order, and where we step in to keep things moving.

Before You Apply

Who Elevate Delaware funding is for

Elevate Delaware is a Delaware Department of Labor workforce-training grant. It's designed for small Delaware employers who want to invest in an employee's skills through an approved training program — like the certificate programs we offer.

We're an approved Elevate Delaware training provider, which means our programs qualify for funding consideration. The funding decision itself, though, is made by the state — we can't guarantee approval or control the state's timeline, and we want to be upfront about that from the start.

The Process

The two steps you need to complete

This is the Delaware Department of Labor's own "Getting Started" sequence for Elevate Delaware — straight off their official flyer, numbered to match. Nothing extra, nothing skipped — just the two official steps, with notes on the part people most often get stuck on.

1
Submit Your Request

Fill out the Employment & Training Services Request Form

This is the official state form that puts your Elevate Delaware request on record. It asks for basic information about you and your employer (employer name, employee count, and that you're subject to Delaware income tax) so the state can confirm you meet the eligibility criteria.

Open the Employment & Training Services Request Form
2
Create Your Account & Register

Set up Delaware JobLink and register for the training

Delaware JobLink is the state's system of record for workforce programs, including Elevate Delaware. Create an account if you don't already have one, then register for the training itself. This is the part people most often get wrong, so take it slow:

When asked for a payment method, choose "Other" — then type "Elevate Delaware" in the comments field. That's what tells the state this registration is an Elevate Delaware funding request rather than a self-pay enrollment.

Common Questions

A few things people usually ask

Do I have to handle the state paperwork myself?
The forms themselves are submitted by you or your employer directly to the state — we're not able to submit them on your behalf. What we do is walk you through each step on a call, flag anything that could cause a delay, and stay in touch until your request is resolved.
How long does Elevate Delaware funding usually take to process?
Processing time is set by the Delaware Department of Labor and can vary. Several weeks is common. That's exactly why we recommend booking a call early — the sooner we know your request is in, the sooner we can help if anything needs attention.
What if my employer has questions about the process?
That's one of the most common reasons requests stall — happy to join a call with you and your employer, or answer their questions directly, so the paperwork moves forward without going back and forth over email.
Does RGP Consulting decide whether I get funded?
No — that decision is made by the Delaware Department of Labor, based on your eligibility and their review. We're an approved training provider, which qualifies our programs for consideration, but the funding determination itself is entirely the state's.
