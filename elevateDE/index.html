Common Questions

What people ask before they apply.

Are these real certificate programs, or just video courses?
Both are defined, noncredit certificate programs with an organized curriculum, weekly live coaching, required checkpoints, a capstone, and a Certificate of Completion — issued only after the completion standards are met, not for attendance alone. Neither is a professional certification, license, or accreditation.
Is coaching individualized, or am I in a big class?
Coaching is delivered one-on-one or in a small group, with size depending on enrollment at the time — never a large fixed cohort. Every participant still receives individualized checkpoint feedback and portfolio review mapped to their own goals.
Is the tuition really all-inclusive?
Yes. Tuition covers all required lessons, coaching sessions, templates, checkpoint feedback, your capstone, and program support — with no required textbook or separate materials fee. Optional third-party services (like custom website work) are billed separately only if agreed to in writing.
Does support continue after the program ends?
Yes, though the shape differs by program. The Entrepreneurship Certificate includes 90 days of Post-Launch Support plus 12 months of continued curriculum and community access. A More Confident You™ includes 90 days of access to the complete digital resource bundle.
Can Elevate Delaware funding help cover tuition?
RGP Consulting LLC is an approved Elevate Delaware training provider. Funding eligibility is determined by the Delaware Department of Labor Workforce Development Board — check with your Elevate Delaware career counselor about your specific eligibility.
