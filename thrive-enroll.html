Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know.

Can I do this while working a full-time job?
+

Yes. THRIVE was designed for busy professionals, parents, and side-hustlers who have real responsibilities. The lessons are structured so you can move through the program in manageable steps without needing to quit your job or overhaul your life.

What if I don't have a business idea yet?
+

You do not need to arrive with a fully formed business idea. THRIVE helps you identify, refine, validate, and shape your idea into something practical and launchable.

What if I am not tech-savvy?
+

You do not need to be a technology expert. THRIVE focuses on practical business development, simple tools, and step-by-step implementation. Premium students also receive additional support and guidance through coaching.

What makes this different from YouTube?
+

YouTube gives you scattered information. THRIVE gives you a structured business launch pathway, organized tools, implementation steps, and a clear progression from idea to launch.

What makes this different from ChatGPT or AI tools?
+

AI can help generate ideas, but it cannot replace a proven framework, accountability, human feedback, community, lived experience, and guided implementation. THRIVE helps you know what to do, when to do it, and why it matters.

Should I choose Essentials or Premium?
+

Choose Essentials if you are a self-starter who wants the complete system and can implement independently. Choose Premium if you want coaching, accountability, feedback, and support as you build and launch.

Can I upgrade from Essentials to Premium later?
+

Yes. Essentials students may upgrade to Premium, subject to availability. If upgrading within the approved upgrade window, their Essentials tuition may be applied toward the Premium enrollment balance.

How does the scholarship process work?
+

Scholarship applicants complete a short application sharing their business idea, motivation, current barriers, and commitment level. Scholarship seats are limited and awarded based on demonstrated readiness, potential, and commitment.

Is there a guarantee?
+

Yes. THRIVE includes a 30-day action-based guarantee. If you complete the required work and demonstrate implementation effort within the first 30 days, we will honor the guarantee according to the stated terms.

Are payment plans available?
+

Yes. We want to make sure cost is never the barrier between you and building something real. At checkout, a number of Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) options are available so you can get started today and spread your payments over time. Simply select your preferred payment option at the enrollment checkout page.

Who is this not for?
+

THRIVE is not for someone looking for shortcuts, passive income promises, or instant results without effort. This program is for serious aspiring entrepreneurs who are willing to think clearly, do the work, and take consistent action.
