Crystal Lugo
Co-Founder · THRIVE Business Idea Launch Accelerator™

Crystal's journey began not as a seasoned entrepreneur, but as a bank teller with an idea.

What started as a simple observation grew into GloveScaler®, a patented product that has earned national recognition, opened doors to pitch opportunities on ABC's hit show Shark Tank, and positioned the product for global distribution.

Through that journey, Crystal learned that breakthrough ideas don't belong exclusively to inventors, CEOs, or industry experts. They often begin with ordinary people who are willing to take a chance on themselves and pursue a solution they believe in.

Today, she helps aspiring entrepreneurs see the potential in their own ideas, navigate the challenges of bringing them to life, and discover that the distance between an idea and a business may be much shorter than they think.

Honest answers

Things people ask first.

What is THRIVE Business Idea Launch Accelerator?
+

THRIVE is a 12-week life coaching and business acceleration experience. It combines mentorship, frameworks, AI tools, community, and accountability to help builders move from idea to launch. It's not a course you watch — it's a program you do.

Is this for people who already have a business idea?
+

Yes — and also for people who don't yet. We help you discover, validate, and shape your idea before we help you build it. Whether you're starting from scratch or sitting on something you've been working on for months, the program meets you where you are.

I have a full-time job. Will I have time for this?
+

The program is designed for busy professionals, working parents, and side-hustlers. Coaching sessions are scheduled to work with real lives, and the templates and AI tools are built specifically to compress the work — so a few focused hours each week can move you further than weeks of trying alone.

What does THRIVE actually stand for?
+

We reveal the full framework inside the FREE Masterclass. It's the operating system that runs through the entire program — and it's worth seeing live.

I'm exploring franchise ownership — is this still right for me?
+

Yes. THRIVE supports both startup founders and people evaluating franchise paths. We help you assess models, weigh tradeoffs, and build the operating mindset and systems you'll need either way.

How is this different from a business course?
+

A course gives you information. THRIVE gives you a guided experience — live coaching, mentorship, accountability, a private community, and structured execution. You're not watching alone. You're building alongside coaches and other founders.

What does the Founders Cohort discount include?
+

Founding members of the first cohort receive 10% off when using the code THRIVE at checkout — plus lifetime founders' pricing on future programs and priority access to mentor sessions.

What if I'm not sure yet?
+

Start with the FREE Masterclass or download the FREE Idea Accelerator Resource. Both give you a real taste of how we work — at no cost — so you can decide if THRIVE is the right next step.
